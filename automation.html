Automating Onboarding Notifications

A working Zapier demonstration connects a Google Form to Gmail. Google Forms records submissions in its linked Google Sheet, while Zapier routes notifications according to the employee's equipment and system-access requirements.

  • Zapier
  • Paths by Zapier
  • Google Forms
  • Google Sheets
  • Gmail
← Back to the NexaPoint project

How the workflow runs

  1. Google Forms: New Form ResponseHR submits employee details and onboarding requirements.
  2. Google Sheets: response recordGoogle Forms automatically stores the submitted response in the linked spreadsheet.
  3. Gmail: HR notificationAn acknowledgement and notification is sent for every new submission.
  4. Paths by Zapier: conditional routingBranch conditions decide whether IT and Procurement receive additional notifications.

Who is notified, and when

Notification rules by department
DepartmentReceives a notification
HREvery onboarding request.
ITWhen equipment OR system access is required.
ProcurementWhen equipment is required.

Testing note: The Zapier trigger and email actions were tested using fictional form entries. My own Gmail address was used to simulate departmental recipients. This is a demonstration workflow, not a live deployment inside NexaPoint or a real employer.
