Project 01 · Healthcare technology · Fictional project simulation

MediConnect Hospital Management System

A proposed web-based hospital management system designed around a scenario in which paper-based records and manual administrative processes create delays in patient registration, appointment handling, medical-record retrieval and billing.

Project CoordinationBusiness AnalysisRequirementsScope ManagementUAT Planning

Problem

The scenario assumed fragmented paper records, slow file retrieval, manual appointment administration, billing-entry errors and limited information sharing between departments.

Proposed solution

Plan an MVP covering patient registration and profiles, appointments, authorised medical-record access, billing, role-based accounts and management reporting.

My role

Project Coordinator / Business Analyst: project charter, requirements drafting, MoSCoW prioritisation, WBS, schedule planning, status reporting, risk and issue management, UAT and handover preparation.

Project 02 · Featured case study · Fictional project simulation

NexaPoint Employee Onboarding Process Improvement

A self-directed project simulation exploring how a growing technology-services company could improve its employee onboarding process, clarify departmental responsibilities and reduce avoidable delays through a structured workflow and notification automation.

Project CoordinationBusiness AnalysisProcess ImprovementJiraZapierGoogle Workspace

Problem

The proposed current-state scenario included late employee documents, unclear ownership, delayed equipment and system access, and inconsistent orientation activities.

Solution

Design a structured onboarding process beginning at offer acceptance, with clear task ownership, earlier departmental involvement, milestones and monitoring.

My role

Project Coordinator / Business Analyst: process discovery, requirements, analysis, planning, coordination design, risk tracking and automation prototype.

Project method

From process discovery to delivery planning

The project was organised into practical stages so that the proposed solution was based on an understanding of the process rather than jumping straight to a tool.

Discover

Identify stakeholders, ask discovery questions and document the proposed AS-IS process.

Analyse

Identify process gaps, likely root causes, impacts and improvement opportunities.

Design

Develop the TO-BE workflow, define handoffs and clarify departmental responsibilities.

Plan

Create a six-week schedule, task breakdown, dependencies, RACI matrix and risk/issue controls.

Prototype

Build and test a form-to-email automation with conditional notifications.

Monitor

Define progress reporting, milestone checks, pilot measures and handover activities.

Automation prototype

Automating onboarding notifications

A working Zapier demonstration connects a Google Form to Gmail. Google Forms records submissions in its linked Google Sheet, while Zapier routes notifications according to the employee's equipment and system-access requirements.

1. Google Forms — New Form ResponseHR submits employee details and onboarding requirements.
2. Google Sheets — response recordGoogle Forms automatically stores the submitted response in the linked spreadsheet.
3. Gmail — HR notificationAn acknowledgement/notification is sent for every new submission.
4. Paths by Zapier — conditional routingBranch conditions determine whether IT and Procurement receive additional notifications.

HR

Receives every onboarding request.

IT

Receives a notification when equipment OR system access is required.

Procurement

Receives a notification when equipment is required.

Testing note: The Zapier trigger and email actions were tested using fictional form entries. The user's own Gmail address was used to simulate departmental recipients. This is a demonstration workflow, not a live deployment inside NexaPoint or a real employer.
