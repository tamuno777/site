NexaPoint Employee Onboarding Process Improvement

A self-directed project simulation exploring how a growing technology-services company could improve its employee onboarding process, clarify departmental responsibilities and reduce avoidable delays through a structured workflow and notification automation.

Overview

Problem

The proposed current-state scenario included late employee documents, unclear ownership, delayed equipment and system access, and inconsistent orientation activities.

Solution

Design a structured onboarding process beginning at offer acceptance, with clear task ownership, earlier departmental involvement, milestones and monitoring.

My role

Project Coordinator / Business Analyst: process discovery, requirements, analysis, planning, coordination design, risk tracking and automation prototype.

Project method

The project followed practical stages, so the proposed solution came from understanding the process rather than jumping straight to a tool.

  1. DiscoverIdentify stakeholders, ask discovery questions and document the proposed AS-IS process.
  2. AnalyseIdentify process gaps, likely root causes, impacts and improvement opportunities.
  3. DesignDevelop the TO-BE workflow, define handoffs and clarify departmental responsibilities.
  4. PlanCreate a six-week schedule, task breakdown, dependencies, RACI matrix and risk and issue controls.
  5. PrototypeBuild and test a form-to-email automation with conditional notifications.
  6. MonitorDefine progress reporting, milestone checks, pilot measures and handover activities.

Methods and controls

Process analysis

AS-IS and TO-BE process mapping, gap analysis and root-cause exploration.

Requirements

Stakeholder discovery, requirements documentation and workflow definition.

Planning

Work breakdown, six-week schedule, milestones, task dependencies and Jira tracking.

Governance

RACI responsibility matrix, stakeholder communication and decision and approval points.

Risk and issue management

Likelihood-impact scoring, mitigation, contingency planning, issue ownership and escalation.

Monitoring and evaluation

Progress updates, milestone tracking, proposed measures and pilot review.

Tools used

Jira

Epic, tasks, workflow statuses, timeline, dates and dependencies.

Google Forms & Sheets

Employee onboarding request capture and response tracking.

Zapier & Gmail

Trigger-based email notifications and conditional departmental routing.

Excel

Project tracker, schedule, Gantt view, milestones, dependencies, resources, baseline and risk and issue logs.

Word / Google Docs

Project brief, stakeholder notes, requirements, process analysis and implementation documentation.

Draw.io

Process flow diagrams and workflow communication.

Deliverables

  • Project brief and success measures
  • Stakeholder register and discovery questions
  • AS-IS process and gap and root-cause analysis
  • TO-BE process design
  • Six-week implementation plan and schedule
  • RACI matrix, risk register and issue log
  • Jira project board and timeline
  • Excel project tracker and Gantt workbook
  • Zapier automation prototype and test scenarios

The project plan and proposed success measures describe intended future work. No real employee onboarding was carried out, and no actual operational improvement or percentage reduction has been claimed.
