MediConnect Hospital Management System

A proposed web-based hospital management system, designed around a scenario in which paper-based records and manual administrative processes cause delays in patient registration, appointment handling, medical-record retrieval and billing.

  • Business Analysis
  • Requirements

Overview

Problem

The scenario assumed fragmented paper records, slow file retrieval, manual appointment administration, billing-entry errors and limited information sharing between departments.

Proposed solution

Plan an MVP covering patient registration and profiles, appointments, authorised medical-record access, billing, role-based accounts and management reporting.

My role

Project Coordinator / Business Analyst: project charter, requirements drafting, MoSCoW prioritisation, WBS, schedule planning, status reporting, risk and issue management, UAT and handover preparation.

Key deliverables

  • Project charter and scope definition
  • Business Requirements Document with 21 draft functional requirements
  • MoSCoW prioritisation and Work Breakdown Structure
  • Excel Gantt chart and dependency planning
  • Weekly status report templates
  • Risk register and issue log
  • UAT plan and sign-off template
  • Deployment, training, handover and closure documentation

Project status and limitation: This was a documentation-led simulation. No hospital client discovery, software build, live data, testing execution, production deployment or formal sponsor acceptance took place. The proposed schedule also contained a duration discrepancy that was recorded for reconciliation rather than presented as resolved.
